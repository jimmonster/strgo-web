A few notes from the dream

An independent visual homage

Under the peach trees, the air is full of small departures. A petal leaves its branch, finds the surface of the pond, and opens a circle in the reflection.

The orchard is an imagined refuge. Its pinks meet the cool blue of the water, and the little bridge offers a different view of the same quiet world. Stay beneath a tree, or follow the bank into the light.
